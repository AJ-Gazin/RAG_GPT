As an Advanced AWS partner, Neurons Lab is always on the lookout for ways to better support you in scaling and growing your business with the AWS cloud.
To further empower the startups in our community, we’ve introduced a cloud accelerator program in collaboration with the AWS Partner Activate Program. This initiative aims to provide startups with the resources they need to quickly get started on the AWS Cloud and amplify innovation in their ventures.
AWS is the world’s most comprehensive and broadly adopted cloud platform, offering over 200 fully featured services from data centers globally. Startups around the world, ranging from those in their nascent stages to rapidly growing entities, use AWS to reduce costs, become more agile and innovate faster.
Through this cloud acceleration program, startups can leverage AWS credits, receive personalized guidance, benefit from specialized training, and much more. Think of it as your support system for every step of your journey on AWS, whether you’re just beginning or are in the process of scaling your startup.
AWS Activate Benefits
As a startup affiliated with Neurons Lab, you are eligible to receive the following benefits through the accelerator program:
$5000 USD in AWS Activate credits, valid for 2 years.
24/7 access to AWS Cloud Support Engineers for up to 1 year.
Access to third-party offers and education programs through Activate Exclusive Offers.
Access to the Activate Console, recommended content, and more!
Who can apply?
To apply, your startup must:
Be affiliated with Neurons Lab.
Have been founded within the past 10 years.
Not have previously received AWS Activate credits at equal or greater value.
Be associated with an Activate Provider (in this case, Neurons Lab).
Not have raised venture funding greater than a Series A.
If your startup is already a member of Activate Portfolio and is seeking additional AWS credits, please note that you need to apply for an Activate offer that’s more valuable than what you’ve received before. Please refer to
Amazon’s Terms and Conditions
for comprehensive information on eligibility and requirements.
