6th of March 2024
– Neurons Lab, an AI consultancy that provides end-to-end services for mid and large-sized enterprises, announced today that it has achieved the Amazon Web Services (AWS) Generative AI Competency. This specialization recognizes Neurons Lab as an AWS Partner that helps customers and the AWS Partner Network (APN) drive the advancement of services, tools, and infrastructure pivotal for implementing generative AI technologies.
Achieving the AWS Generative AI Competency differentiates Neurons Lab as an AWS Partner that has demonstrated technical proficiency and proven customer success, supporting clients to develop generative AI solutions to augment their products, operations, and services in a secure, accurate, and impactful way. Neurons Lab possesses the experience and expertise demonstrated through successful projects addressing customer challenges using generative AI solutions. These solutions enable digital transformation strategies for augmenting the customer experience, delivering hyper-personalized and engaging content, streamlining workflows, and delivering actionable results powered by generative AI technology from AWS.
“Neurons Lab is proud to achieve the AWS Generative AI Competency,”
said Alex Honchar, Director of AI Engineering & Partner at Neurons Lab.
“Our team is dedicated to supporting clients to reach their objectives by leveraging the agility, breadth of services, and pace of innovation that AWS provides. With this new AWS Generative AI Competency designation, we hope to continue to meet and surpass our customer’s needs at the cutting edge of generative AI-based innovation.”
The AWS Competency Program aims to assist customers in connecting with AWS Partners who possess extensive knowledge and technical expertise in using AWS technologies and best practices to adopt generative AI. These AWS Partners facilitate the seamless integration and deployment of AWS-based solutions to meet the unique needs of all customers, from startups to global enterprises.
Neurons Lab offers a range of capabilities within the generative AI field, including Conversational AI, Personalized Digital Experience, Intelligent document processing, and Synthetic data creation.
In a recent project, Neurons Lab partnered with ​​
Peak Defence
to enhance their business operations and customer offering using generative AI technologies. Peak Defence provides cutting-edge information security compliance consulting services, helping clients meet standards and frameworks like ISO 27001, NIST, and SOC2. With increased demand, achieving compliance, managing information security systems, and responding to RFPs significantly drained employee time and resources, limiting the company’s scalability.
To evolve its offerings and unlock new capacity, Neurons Lab worked with Peak Defence to implement an AI platform on Amazon Bedrock that leverages LLMs (large language models) to automate manual compliance processes. Peak Defence’s end goal with the project was to fuel its transition from consulting into an SAAS platform for customers. AI has enabled them to transfer and scale their knowledge out for the customers, creating better security and permitting customers to get clarity and act quickly in a sea of data and security threats.
The reason behind using AWS services in particular was to retain control over the customer data and ensure it doesn’t end up in an “uncontrolled” AI learning environment, which is a big concern in the cyber security world.
Early results show the technology is already allowing Peak Defence to make the transition into a SAAS. Ultimately they can take on more customers without expanding headcount.
“We needed a professional and flexible partner while going through a fundamental pivot. Neurons Lab helped us add gen AI capabilities to a successful platform. This enabled Peak Defence to evolve and unlock new capabilities for its customers. Neurons Lab team brings experience, good organization, and creativity to the projects. That combo makes it efficient and fun to work together. We recommend these guys without hesitation.”
Roman Jasins, Co-Founder & Board Member of Peak Defence
