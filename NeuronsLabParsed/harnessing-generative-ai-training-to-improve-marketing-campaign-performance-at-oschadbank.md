Partner Overview
Oschadbank
(Ощадбанк) is the State Savings Bank of Ukraine.
Founded by the Government of Ukraine
, the bank – headquartered in Kyiv – has close to
1,200 branches
, with
UAH 12 billion of operating profit and UAH 9.5 billion profit before tax
in 2023. As confirmed by the National Bank of Ukraine, It is officially one of the country’s
systemically important financial institutions (SIFI)
.
Project Overview
Neurons Lab partnered with Visa to provide the Oschadbank marketing team with a training program
– focused on
understanding and using generative AI to solve key business challenges
.
As an
AWS Advanced Consulting Partner
and
one of the first companies in the world
to receive the
AWS Generative AI Competency
, we delivered a
GenAI Enablement
education program to the Oschadbank marketing team.
The aims were to:
Increase the team’s awareness
of GenAI capabilities and marketing use cases
Provide hands-on experience
in how to use GenAI tools for marketing tasks
Business Challenges
The Oschadbank marketing team wanted to
increase productivity and operational speed
by leveraging GenAI to:
Create marketing content
and analyze data more efficiently
Create more targeted messages
for different audiences
Personalize customer journeys
more effectively
Keep up with competitors
in terms of speed and creativity
However,
challenges for the marketing team to overcome included:
Low awareness
of the different AI solutions available and their use cases in marketing
Low implementation
of cutting-edge AI tools in its marketing strategies and ongoing tasks
Difficulty choosing the right AI tools
for their needs given the crowded market of solutions available
